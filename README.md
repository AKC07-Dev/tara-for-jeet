# Tara's Secret Garden

Build a complete, polished, highly interactive personal website called:

"for jeet, from tara"

This is a PRIVATE DIGITAL GIFT for one specific person.

Do NOT build a generic romantic landing page.

The goal is to create the feeling that Tara secretly built an entire little world specifically for Jeet.

EMOTIONAL GOAL

Jeet is a businessman who had to take on responsibilities much earlier than people his age.

He has sacrificed a lot of normal enjoyment and has experienced betrayal and broken trust from people close to him.

He struggles to open up.

He has been feeling frustrated, exhausted and tired.

Tara genuinely cares about him and wants him to know:

"You don't have to carry everything alone."

The website should communicate:

I care about you.

You matter to me.

You can trust me.

You can rely on me.

You can come to me anytime.

You don't need to pretend you're okay.

You are not a bad person.

Your past doesn't define you.

You deserve happiness.

Better days are coming.

I am here.

BUT:

Do not make the experience depressing.

The emotional progression should be:

MYSTERY → PLAYFULNESS → CUTE → TEASING → WARMTH → EMOTIONAL → REASSURANCE → HOPE → SMILE.

DESIGN STYLE

Create a premium cute-but-elegant visual identity.

Think:

1990s romance + modern editorial website + private digital diary + cute bear stickers.

Color palette:

warm cream

cocoa

muted blush

dusty rose

dark chocolate

soft peach

very subtle lavender

Avoid neon pink.

Avoid childish rainbow colors.

Avoid generic purple-blue AI gradients.

Avoid excessive glassmorphism.

Avoid excessive rounded rectangles.

Avoid generic SaaS UI.

Use elegant serif typography for major emotional statements and modern sans-serif typography for interface elements.

Occasionally use handwritten typography for tiny personal notes.

BEARS

Use cute white Dudu/Bubu/Mocha Bear inspired couple-style stickers.

IMPORTANT:

If actual copyrighted artwork is unavailable, create original simple bear-like SVG/CSS illustrations rather than copying copyrighted images.

Use them sparingly.

Examples:

bear peeking from edge of screen

sleeping bear

bear holding heart

bear holding a tiny sign

two bears sitting together

bear reacting to answers

bear appearing during transitions

They are emotional accents, NOT the entire visual identity.

ENTRY SCREEN

Full-screen cinematic intro.

Text:

"pssst..."

small text:

"this little place is only for one very specific person."

Then:

"Are you Jeet?"

Button:

"yes, obviously"

Password screen:

"Okay then...
prove it."

Password:

Tara

Incorrect password feedback should be playful.

Correct:

"Fine.
You may enter."

Animate the transition beautifully.

Store the login state locally.

This is not security-critical authentication.

EXPERIENCE FLOW

There should be NO normal navigation bar.

Instead, the website should feel like a guided interactive story.

Use progress indicators subtly, such as:

01 / 09

or tiny stars.

01 — THE RULES

Question cards.

Examples:

"Do you agree that you deserve happiness?"

"Do you agree that taking care of everyone doesn't mean forgetting yourself?"

"Do you agree that Tara is allowed to annoy you indefinitely?"

"Do you accept that you are loved?"

Give humorous responses based on answers.

Don't allow the experience to feel like a boring form.

02 — EMOTIONAL INTERROGATION

Create an interactive quiz.

Question examples:

"When something bothers you, what do you usually do?"

Options should humorously represent:

talk

overthink

say I'm fine

work until you forget

After several questions:

"Diagnosis:
you are ridiculously bad at admitting when you're tired."

Then:

"Luckily, Tara knows."

03 — THINGS I NOTICE

A cinematic scroll section.

Reveal individual lines as the user scrolls/clicks:

"I know you don't always say when you're tired."

"I know you've had to grow up faster than you should have."

"I know trusting people hasn't always been easy."

"I know you carry more than you tell people."

"And I know you probably don't realize how important you are to me."

Use beautiful typography and subtle animation.

04 — OPEN WHEN...

Create an interactive "drawer" or grid.

Cards:

OPEN WHEN YOU'RE TIRED

OPEN WHEN YOU'RE ANGRY

OPEN WHEN YOU FEEL ALONE

OPEN WHEN YOU DON'T TRUST ANYONE

OPEN WHEN YOU THINK YOU'RE NOT DOING ENOUGH

OPEN WHEN YOU NEED TO SMILE

Each opens a small personalized message.

Keep messages short and sincere.

05 — THE CONSTELLATION

Create an interactive dark night-sky section.

Stars represent little truths.

Click stars to reveal:

"Someone is proud of you."

"Someone wants you to rest."

"Someone believes in you."

"Someone wants to hear your nonsense."

"Someone wants you to be happy."

"Someone is here."

When enough stars are clicked, they connect into a subtle heart/constellation.

06 — DO NOT PRESS

Huge playful button:

"DO NOT PRESS"

He obviously presses it.

On click:

confetti + bears + tiny hearts + playful messages.

Examples:

"Why did you listen to the website?"

"Jeet."

"You really cannot resist buttons."

"Okay fine. One more."

After multiple clicks:

"I made this just to make you smile."

07 — READ THIS SLOWLY

Slow emotional section.

One sentence appears at a time.

"You don't have to explain everything."

"You don't have to be strong around me."

"You don't have to pretend."

"You don't have to earn your place in my life."

"You can come to me when everything feels too much."

"You can also come to me when absolutely nothing is wrong."

"You can talk."

"You can stay quiet."

"You can be yourself."

"I'll still be here."

08 — THE LETTER

Do not show the letter until this point.

Show an elegant envelope.

Text:

"There's something I couldn't turn into a button."

Button:

"open it"

Animate envelope opening.

Then reveal a beautiful handwritten-style letter from Tara to Jeet.

The letter should communicate the emotional message described above.

Use realistic conversational language rather than dramatic poetry.

09 — THE PROMISE

Final interaction.

"One tiny promise?"

Reveal:

"Please don't forget to take care of yourself while taking care of everyone else."

Buttons:

"Deal 🤝"

"I'll think about it"

If "I'll think about it":

"That's a very businessman answer."

Then show Deal again.

FINAL SCREEN

Dark warm night.

Very subtle stars.

Two tiny original bear characters.

Text appears slowly:

"Whatever happens..."

"however heavy life gets..."

"you will always have a place here."

Then:

"Come back whenever you need."

Finally:

"— Tara"

A small:

"start again"

button.

MICROINTERACTIONS

Implement:

smooth page transitions

button hover reactions

spring animations

text reveal animations

subtle parallax

cursor interactions where appropriate

tiny floating particles

bear reactions

confetti

envelope animation

constellation connections

scroll-triggered reveals

playful incorrect-answer responses

section completion transitions

Do not animate everything.

Animation should create emotion, not visual noise.

MOBILE

Mobile-first.

This is extremely important.

The website will probably be opened from a phone.

Make every interaction comfortable with touch.

No hover-only functionality.

No tiny buttons.

No horizontal overflow.

No animation that destroys mobile performance.

TECH STACK

Use:

React
TypeScript
Tailwind CSS
Framer Motion / Motion
Lucide icons
SVG/CSS illustrations

Use reusable components.

Create a clean state machine for the journey rather than manually duplicating logic.

Use localStorage for the login/session state.

No backend is necessary unless absolutely required.

QUALITY BAR

Before finishing, inspect the entire experience and remove anything that looks like:

generic AI website

SaaS template

template navbar

generic hero section

stock image

generic card grid

meaningless statistics

excessive gradients

childish design

excessive glassmorphism

excessive rounded cards

giant text everywhere

unnecessary sections

The final product should feel:

PERSONAL
INTENTIONAL
CINEMATIC
WARM
PLAYFUL
PREMIUM
EMOTIONAL
MEMORABLE

Most importantly:

It should feel like Tara spent hours making this specifically for Jeet.

Do not stop after creating a basic prototype.

Build the complete polished experience.
